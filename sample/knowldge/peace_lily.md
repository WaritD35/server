# Peace lily (Spathiphyllum) — visual care reference

## Healthy appearance

A healthy Peace lily displays lush, abundant foliage that creates a full, bushy silhouette. The leaves of a healthy Peace lily exhibit a deep, glossy green coloration across the entire leaf blade. A healthy plant features leaf stems that stand firm and rigid near the soil line. The foliage naturally arches gracefully outward and downward as the leaves extend from the center of the plant. A beginner might mistake the natural, gentle downward curve of mature leaves for wilting. The natural downward curve of mature leaves is entirely normal as long as the supporting stems remain stiff and upright. A thriving Peace lily frequently produces tall, slender green stalks topped with bright white, hood-like modified leaves called spathes. The central flower spike inside the white spathe displays a bumpy texture and a pale yellow coloration. The white flower spathes naturally slowly transition to a pale green color as the flowers age over several weeks. A beginner might mistakenly worry about an occasional single old leaf at the very bottom of the plant turning yellow and drying up. The occasional yellowing of a single oldest bottom leaf is a harmless, natural part of the plant shedding outdated foliage. The visible surface of the potting soil typically appears dark and slightly textured without any thick white crusts or fluffy molds.

## Problems

### Severe Wilting

* Care need: water_deficit
* Visible signs: The entire leaf canopy droops dramatically toward the pot rim. The leaf stems bend downward near the soil rather than standing upright.
* Healthy contrast: Healthy leaf stems stand rigidly upward before arching outward at the leaf blade.
* Not to be confused with: Severe wilting should not be confused with the natural gentle curve of a healthy leaf blade. A natural gentle curve only affects the leaf blade itself, whereas severe wilting causes the supporting stem to bend downward.
* Likely cause: The potting soil has dried out completely.
* Action: Pour water evenly over the soil surface until water freely runs out the bottom drainage holes of the pot. Empty the saucer beneath the pot after thirty minutes to prevent standing water.
* Urgency: today
* Expected recovery: The bent leaf stems will lift and stand upright again, typically within 4 hours, at most 24 hours.

### Brown Leaf Tips

* Care need: low_humidity
* Visible signs: The sharply pointed tips of the leaves turn dry, brown, and brittle. The brown discoloration may extend slightly along the outermost leaf edges.
* Healthy contrast: Healthy leaf tips taper to a point while remaining entirely dark green and physically flexible.
* Not to be confused with: Brown leaf tips should not be confused with completely yellow older leaves. Entirely yellow older leaves indicate age or watering issues, whereas brown tips specifically affect only the outermost point of otherwise green leaves.
* Likely cause: The surrounding indoor air is too dry. Mineral buildup from tap water can also contribute to brown leaf tips.
* Action: Place a wide tray filled with pebbles and water directly underneath the plant pot. Ensure the bottom of the pot sits securely on the pebbles and does not touch the underlying water. Use filtered water or rainwater for future waterings.
* Urgency: this month
* Expected recovery: Existing brown leaf tips will never turn green again, but new leaves will emerge completely green without brown tips, typically within 4 weeks, at most 8 weeks.

### Widespread Yellowing of Lower Leaves

* Care need: overwatering
* Visible signs: Multiple leaves located near the base of the plant turn a solid, bright yellow color simultaneously. The yellowing lower leaves may eventually develop soft brown patches in the center of the yellow areas.
* Healthy contrast: The lower leaves of a thriving Peace lily match the dark green shade of the upper leaves.
* Not to be confused with: Widespread yellowing of lower leaves should not be confused with the occasional death of a single old leaf. The occasional death of a single old leaf is a normal aging process, whereas overwatering causes several lower leaves to turn yellow at the exact same time.
* Likely cause: The soil remains constantly wet, preventing the plant roots from absorbing necessary oxygen.
* Action: Stop watering the plant immediately. Wait until the top surface of the soil appears visibly dry and lighter in color before adding any more water.
* Urgency: this week
* Expected recovery: The currently yellowed lower leaves will not recover, but the spread of yellowing to healthy green leaves will stop, typically within 1 week, at most 2 weeks.

### Bleached or Scorched Leaves

* Care need: excess_light
* Visible signs: The uppermost leaves directly facing the light source appear pale green, faded, or develop large yellow-white patches. The faded yellow-white patches on upper leaves look washed out rather than bright yellow.
* Healthy contrast: The uppermost leaves display a rich, uniform dark green color across the entire leaf surface.
* Not to be confused with: Bleached upper leaves should not be confused with the widespread yellowing of lower leaves caused by overwatering. Bleached upper leaves occur exclusively on the top canopy exposed to light, whereas overwatering primarily affects the lower, older leaves near the soil.
* Likely cause: Direct sunlight is hitting the foliage and physically burning the leaf tissue.
* Action: Move the plant pot away from the brightly lit window. Place the plant pot in a location where the incoming light casts a blurry shadow rather than a sharply defined shadow.
* Urgency: this week
* Expected recovery: Severely bleached leaves will not regain dark green coloration, but the plant will start producing healthy dark green new leaves, typically within 3 weeks, at most 6 weeks.

### Green Emerging Flowers

* Care need: low_light
* Visible signs: Newly emerging flower spathes open with a distinctly pale green color instead of white. The plant produces very few or zero new flowers over a span of several months.
* Healthy contrast: Newly emerging flower spathes open as a brilliant, solid white color and remain pristine white for several weeks.
* Not to be confused with: Newly emerging green flowers should not be confused with the natural aging process of old flowers. Old flowers naturally turn green as the blooms die over several weeks, whereas light-deprived new flowers are green from the moment the buds unfurl.
* Likely cause: The plant is not receiving enough ambient light energy to produce brilliant white blooms.
* Action: Move the plant pot closer to a natural light source. Ensure the plant receives bright indirect light where a person could easily read a book without turning on artificial lighting.
* Urgency: this month
* Expected recovery: The currently green flowers will not turn white, but newly emerging flowers will open stark white, typically within 4 weeks, at most 12 weeks.

### White Cotton-like Patches on Stems

* Care need: pest
* Visible signs: Small, fuzzy white masses that resemble tiny pieces of cotton appear nestled in the crevices where the leaf stems meet the main base of the plant. A sticky, shiny residue may also be visible on the upper surfaces of lower leaves directly beneath the white masses.
* Healthy contrast: The crevices where the leaf stems meet the main base are perfectly clean and completely free of any white fuzz or webbing.
* Not to be confused with: White cotton-like patches should not be confused with the naturally white flower spathes. The naturally white flower spathes are large, distinct structures on tall stalks, whereas the pest patches are tiny, shapeless blobs hiding in tight stem crevices.
* Likely cause: Mealybugs have infested the plant and are actively feeding on the plant sap.
* Action: Wipe the visible white cotton-like patches off the stems using a cotton swab dipped in standard rubbing alcohol. Spray the entire visible plant foliage with a gentle insecticidal soap designed specifically for houseplants.
* Urgency: today
* Expected recovery: The visible white patches will disappear immediately upon removal, and no new white cotton-like patches will appear on the stems, typically within 1 week, at most 3 weeks.

### Blackened Leaf Edges

* Care need: temperature_stress
* Visible signs: Large patches along the edges of the leaves turn dark black and become visually dry. The black discoloration often appears abruptly on the outer leaves nearest to windows or doors.
* Healthy contrast: The edges of a healthy Peace lily leaf maintain a vibrant green color right up to the very margin.
* Not to be confused with: Blackened leaf edges should not be confused with the brown leaf tips caused by dry air. Brown leaf tips are specifically light brown and confined solely to the point of the leaf, whereas blackened leaf edges cover broader areas along the sides of the leaf in a much darker shade.
* Likely cause: The plant has been exposed to a freezing draft from a window or an air conditioning vent.
* Action: Relocate the plant pot to a stable indoor environment away from drafty doors, drafty windows, and heating or cooling vents. Prune away the severely blackened leaves using clean household scissors.
* Urgency: this week
* Expected recovery: The black sections will not heal, but the remaining green foliage will stop developing new black patches, typically within 1 week, at most 2 weeks.
