# Money tree (Pachira aquatica) — visual care reference

## Healthy appearance

A healthy Money tree displays vibrant, bright green leaves grouped in umbrella-like clusters of five to seven leaflets. The leaves of a healthy Money tree stand outward and slightly upward from the supporting green stems. A healthy Money tree typically features a sturdy, upright trunk, which is often commercially braided from several individual main stems. The trunks of a healthy Money tree appear firm and woody with a greenish-brown bark at the base leading to flexible green stems at the top. A beginner might mistake the naturally lighter green color of newly emerging leaves for a nutrient deficiency. The light green color of newly emerging leaves is completely normal, and the newly emerging leaves will darken to match the older foliage as the foliage matures. A beginner might also mistake the occasional yellowing and dropping of a single very old leaf near the bottom of the canopy for a disease. The shedding of a single old lower leaf is a natural part of the Money tree's growth cycle as the plant focuses energy on upper canopy growth. The visible surface of the potting soil typically appears dark and slightly textured without heavy white mineral crusts or excessive mold growth.

## Problems

### Widespread Yellowing of Lower Leaves

* Care need: overwatering
* Visible signs: Multiple lower leaves turn a bright, solid yellow color at the same time and begin to droop significantly. The solid yellow leaves often detach from the main stem and fall onto the soil surface while the fallen leaves are still completely yellow.
* Healthy contrast: The lower leaves of a healthy Money tree maintain the same vibrant green color as the rest of the upper canopy.
* Not to be confused with: Widespread yellowing of lower leaves should not be confused with the natural shedding of a single old leaf. The natural shedding of an old leaf happens one leaf at a time over many months, whereas overwatering causes several leaves to turn yellow simultaneously.
* Likely cause: The potting soil stays consistently wet for too long, depriving the roots of necessary oxygen.
* Action: Withhold water entirely until the top surface of the potting soil appears visibly lighter in color and completely dry. Ensure the drainage saucer beneath the plant pot contains zero standing water.
* Urgency: this week
* Expected recovery: The currently yellow leaves will fall off and will not recover, but the yellowing process will stop spreading to healthy green leaves, typically within 1 week, at most 2 weeks.

### Severely Drooping Canopy with Shriveling Trunk

* Care need: water_deficit
* Visible signs: The entire leaf canopy droops dramatically downward toward the floor. The green stems supporting the leaves become limp, and the woody base of the braided trunk shows deep vertical wrinkles or a shrunken appearance.
* Healthy contrast: The leaf stems of a healthy Money tree stand rigidly outward, and the main woody trunk appears smooth and plump without any vertical wrinkles.
* Not to be confused with: A severely drooping canopy with a shriveling trunk should not be confused with a plant bending toward a light source. A plant bending toward light simply angles all stiff growth in one direction, whereas a water deficit causes the entire plant to look limp and deflated in all directions.
* Likely cause: The potting soil has become completely bone dry throughout the entire plant pot.
* Action: Pour water evenly over the top of the soil until a steady stream of water exits the bottom drainage holes of the plant pot. Empty the saucer beneath the plant pot thirty minutes after the initial watering.
* Urgency: today
* Expected recovery: The limp green stems will stiffen and lift the leaves back to a normal upright posture, typically within 12 hours, at most 48 hours.

### Brown Crispy Leaf Edges

* Care need: low_humidity
* Visible signs: The outermost edges and sharply pointed tips of the leaves turn brown, dry, and brittle. The brown, dry tissue often crumbles easily and is surrounded by a very thin yellow margin separating the brown edge from the healthy green center.
* Healthy contrast: The leaf edges of a healthy Money tree remain entirely green, smooth, and physically flexible right to the very tip.
* Not to be confused with: Brown crispy leaf edges should not be confused with completely yellow and drooping leaves caused by overwatering. Overwatering turns the entire leaf body yellow and soft, whereas low humidity causes only the margins to become brown and brittle while the center remains green.
* Likely cause: The ambient indoor air is excessively dry, often due to indoor heating systems or air conditioning units.
* Action: Place a wide tray filled with pebbles and water directly underneath the plant pot. Ensure the bottom of the plant pot rests firmly on the dry pebbles and does not submerge into the underlying water in the tray.
* Urgency: this month
* Expected recovery: The existing brown leaf edges will never turn green again, but newly forming leaves will grow without brown, crispy edges, typically within 3 weeks, at most 6 weeks.

### Sudden Dropping of Completely Green Leaves

* Care need: temperature_stress
* Visible signs: Large numbers of perfectly green, healthy-looking leaves suddenly detach from the plant and fall onto the soil surface. The fallen green leaves show no obvious signs of yellowing, browning, or wilting prior to dropping off the plant.
* Healthy contrast: A healthy Money tree retains completely green leaves firmly attached to the main supporting stems.
* Not to be confused with: The sudden dropping of completely green leaves should not be confused with the dropping of yellow leaves caused by overwatering. Overwatering causes the leaves to change color entirely to yellow before falling, whereas temperature stress causes leaves to drop while the leaves still look vibrant and green.
* Likely cause: The plant has been exposed to a sudden cold draft from an open window, an exterior door, or an air conditioning vent.
* Action: Move the plant pot to a stable indoor location far away from drafty windows, exterior doors, and active heating or cooling vents. Keep the plant pot in a room where temperatures remain consistently comfortable for a human.
* Urgency: this week
* Expected recovery: The fallen green leaves cannot be reattached to the plant, but the sudden dropping of green foliage will completely cease, typically within 1 week, at most 2 weeks.

### Pale Bleached Patches on Upper Leaves

* Care need: excess_light
* Visible signs: The leaves located at the very top of the plant canopy develop large, washed-out, white or pale yellow patches. The faded patches appear directly on the upward-facing surfaces of the leaves nearest to the primary light source.
* Healthy contrast: The upper leaves of a healthy Money tree display a rich, uniform green color across the entire top surface of the leaf canopy.
* Not to be confused with: Pale bleached patches on upper leaves should not be confused with the naturally lighter green color of brand new growth. Brand new growth is uniformly light green across the entire newly formed leaf, whereas bleached patches appear as irregular, faded spots on otherwise mature, dark green leaves.
* Likely cause: Direct, harsh sunlight is hitting the uppermost foliage and physically scorching the leaf tissue.
* Action: Relocate the plant pot further away from the brightly lit window. Position the plant pot in a spot where the incoming sunlight casts a soft, blurry shadow rather than a sharp, distinct shadow.
* Urgency: this week
* Expected recovery: The severely bleached leaf patches will not regain normal dark green color, but the plant will start producing healthy green leaves without faded spots, typically within 2 weeks, at most 4 weeks.

### Leggy Stems with Undersized Leaves

* Care need: low_light
* Visible signs: The plant produces noticeably long, thin green stems with unusually large gaps between the leaf clusters. The new leaves that emerge on these excessively long stems are significantly smaller and paler than the older leaves located lower down on the plant.
* Healthy contrast: A healthy Money tree produces compact growth with robust stems and large, broad leaflets that grow relatively close together.
* Not to be confused with: Leggy stems with undersized leaves should not be confused with a severely drooping canopy caused by a water deficit. A drooping canopy points straight downward due to a lack of water, whereas leggy stems grow upward but appear stretched, thin, and visibly sparse.
* Likely cause: The plant is positioned in a dark corner and is actively stretching to find a stronger source of ambient light.
* Action: Move the plant pot significantly closer to a window that provides bright, indirect sunlight. Ensure the plant pot is placed in a room bright enough to easily read a printed book without turning on an electric light.
* Urgency: this month
* Expected recovery: The currently stretched stems will remain long, but brand new stems will grow thicker and much more compact, typically within 4 weeks, at most 8 weeks.

### Fine Webbing and Pale Speckled Leaves

* Care need: pest
* Visible signs: Extremely fine, tiny white webs stretch between the individual leaflets or bridge the gaps where the leaves meet the stems. The leaves covered by the fine webbing develop a dull, dusty appearance with hundreds of tiny yellow or white specks across the green surface.
* Healthy contrast: The stems and leaves of a healthy Money tree are entirely free of any webbing and display a smooth, completely solid green color without any tiny specks.
* Not to be confused with: Fine webbing and pale speckled leaves should not be confused with normal household dust collecting on the leaves. Normal household dust easily wipes away leaving a solid green leaf underneath, whereas pest damage leaves permanent pale stippling marks directly within the leaf tissue.
* Likely cause: Spider mites have infested the plant foliage and are actively piercing the leaf tissue to feed on the interior plant sap.
* Action: Wipe down all visible leaf surfaces and stems with a damp cloth to physically remove the spider mite webbing. Spray the entire visible plant canopy thoroughly with a commercial horticultural oil or an insecticidal soap designed specifically for houseplants.
* Urgency: today
* Expected recovery: The visible webbing will vanish immediately upon wiping, and no new webbing will appear on the plant stems, typically within 1 week, at most 3 weeks.
